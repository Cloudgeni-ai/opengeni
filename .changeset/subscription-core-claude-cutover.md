Claude subscriptions move onto the shared subscription core in one drained maintenance migration (0740), on the same receipt, readiness and parity report as the SuperGrok cutover. Every organization, workspace and personal Claude account, OAuth login or setup token, becomes a shared-core connection with one readable secret copy; pins, rotation settings, current usage with its per-model cooldowns, live leases, waiting turns and their pending wakes, each live turn's spent token refresh and the accepted authority of queued, scheduled and inbox work carry over with exact parity, and any mismatch aborts the migration with nothing changed. OAuth logins and setup tokens are never merged. User-scoped accounts become their owner's personal connections, and the six legacy Claude tables, including the usage table, become read-only. A binary that includes 0740 refuses to start until the cutover has committed. The SuperGrok and Claude cutovers run in one maintenance window or separately.
