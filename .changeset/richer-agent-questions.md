Agents can mark one option of a question as recommended, ask a yes/no question whose two buttons use their own words (`confirm: true` with two options, the action first), and attach a short preview to each option, such as the exact text a choice would send. The web and phone question cards show a "Recommended" badge, the two decision buttons and the previews.
