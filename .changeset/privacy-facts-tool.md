Agents answer privacy, retention, training and "where does it run" questions from server facts. The new always-visible `privacy_facts` tool reports the chat's model, provider route and how it is paid for, the route's declared region, whether the workspace is EU-only, the provider's training, retention and processing location from one reviewed table with source links and the date checked, Opengeni's own retention facts, and the deployment's privacy policy, terms and support contact. Unknowns are stated as unknown. The operational contract tells agents to call it for these questions and answer only from it.
