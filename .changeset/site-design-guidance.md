New apps look designed instead of generic. The bundled `sites` Skill now carries design guidance (`references/design.md`) that the agent reads before it writes a new app or restyles one: what the first screen leads with, how to compose a tool in the Opengeni look, how to design a page of its own, the states every app needs, and the generated-page habits to leave out. A page shown to others (a landing page, invitation, menu, portfolio) now gets a design of its own instead of the Opengeni look. A person's or workspace's own design still comes first.
