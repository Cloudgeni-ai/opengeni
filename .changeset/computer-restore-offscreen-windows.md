On macOS, computer targets no longer list windows a person never sees: a headless browser's page windows (including the browser from browser_open), and windows an app keeps ordered out such as closed popovers. A minimized window or a window of a hidden app is listed as readable, and reading it first brings it back on screen behind the person's front window, without activating its app or taking focus. Only a window on another Space, such as a full-screen app, stays marked off screen.
