Operators can now see turns parked waiting for subscription capacity. A turn that finds no account with capacity (a usage limit or no eligible account) parks as its chat's active turn, so `opengeni_turns_queued_by_hold{hold="waiting_capacity"}`, which counts only prompts queued behind it, read 0 while turns waited for hours. The control worker now publishes `opengeni_turns_waiting_capacity{provider}`, the turns parked right now that run as soon as capacity returns, by the provider they wait on (`codex`, `claude`, `xai`; 0 when none), and `opengeni_turns_waiting_capacity_oldest_age_seconds{provider}`, the seconds since the oldest of them parked. A wait in a paused or stopped chat (its own Pause or Stop, an ancestor's or the workspace's) or one the capacity reconcile would supersede is not counted. The worker logs `Turn started waiting for subscription capacity` when a turn parks and `Turn stopped waiting for subscription capacity` (with `outcome`, `reason` and `waitedSeconds`) when capacity returns or the wait is superseded.

Migration 1485 extends the already granted `opengeni_private.summarize_queued_turns_by_hold()` with these rows (an earlier worker ignores them), so a normal deploy publishes the gauges with no further migration. The summary now reads inside the session inventory window, and `subscription_capacity_waiters` and `workspace_inference_controls` gain the owner-only inventory read policy sessions and turns already have: under a database owner that does not bypass row security, the queued-turn split also counts queued turns and workspace pauses it used to miss. `@opengeni/db` exports `summarizeTurnHolds`, `summarizeCapacityWaitingTurns` and `CAPACITY_WAITING_TURN_PROVIDERS`, and a resumed or superseded capacity reconcile reports when the turn parked.
