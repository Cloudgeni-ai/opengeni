Sub-agent chats you work in are now easy to get back to. Control+Tab lists every chat you opened recently, sub-agent chats included (the chats it carries on with after those stay your own). In ⌘K, recently opened sub-agent chats join Recent chats, and a search lists up to three matching sub-agent chats in their own group below the chats, including ones only the server found; the server's chat search now covers top-level chats separately, so a workspace full of sub-agent chats cannot push them out. Pinning a sub-agent chat makes it one of your own: it lists like any other chat in ⌘K, Control+Tab and the home screens' pinned chats. Wherever a sub-agent chat shows in these lists it says which chat it belongs to ("in Release lead"); the app reads the parent's title when it doesn't have it and remembers it with your recent chats.
