Failures no longer show people text that was written for operators. When a turn fails with an unclassified error, or with one of the few codes whose recorded text is an operator diagnostic (`pre_claim_failure`, `interrupted_turn_retired`, `worker_death_redispatch_exhausted`, `sandbox_command_observation_unavailable`, `sandbox_command_start_outcome_unknown`, `sandbox_materialization_verification_failed`, `invalid_sse_terminal`), the timeline names the outcome instead: the startup step the turn failed at, in that step's own words ("Sandbox didn't start"), or "The turn stopped unexpectedly". The recorded text stays reachable: as the turn chip's hover title, behind Details on a failed turn's notice, and behind Details on the failed startup step, which now expands to show its recorded error (except under `toolDetails="plain"`). The failed-session banner uses the same rule and says "The sandbox didn't start." when that is what happened. Copy the worker writes for people (coded provider, subscription, MCP and other failures) is unchanged, and stored events, including the text the agent reads, are untouched.

Because the projection now presents the failure once, `TurnEndItem.failureText` and the timeline groups' `failureText` hold the people-facing summary, with the replaced text in the new optional `failureDetail`; `TurnSummary` takes a matching `failureDetail` prop. React Native and other renderers of `failureText` get the plain summary without changes. `StartupPhaseItem` gains an optional `detail`, and `presentFailure` returns a `diagnostic` flag.
