Organization owners and admins can turn Personal workspaces off under Security & data. Off hides them from the workspace switcher and landing, refuses new chats and schedules there, and routes Slack direct messages to a shared workspace; each person's machines, browsers and vaults keep working from shared workspaces, and existing chats stay reachable.
