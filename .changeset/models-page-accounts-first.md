Organization settings > Models now leads with Accounts, so admins see right away what is connected, who it's available to and which accounts need reconnecting. Workspace-owned accounts say which workspace they belong to, and organization Claude accounts show where they're available, like Codex and SuperGrok. "How accounts are used" has one row per provider instead of stacked dropdowns, keeps dependent rules nested under the rule they depend on, and moves which rules workspaces can change to its own page instead of a lock link on every rule. The per-workspace rules table is gone; each workspace row now says only what it sets differently from the organization. The Codex extra-credit balance is formatted as a number.
