A person can now have the agent do in the chat what they would otherwise do in settings: switch this chat's model, invite someone or share the chat, check their plan, credits and usage, or change a workspace preference. In a chat a person started, `admin_actions_search`, `admin_action_describe` and `admin_action_call` are listed by default and run as the person who sent the message, never with more than that message itself could do: what it may do is frozen with its turn when it is accepted, capped to the chat's workspace. Reads run at once; every change first shows that person a plain confirmation card in the chat ("Invite anna@example.com to your organization as an admin?", one line of the key values, Allow and Cancel, the raw call behind Details), and the API refuses a change that person did not confirm. Only someone signed in to the app itself can confirm changes; with any delegated sign-in (an embed's host-issued token, an external user, another integration's person token) the route only reads unless the deployment sets `OPENGENI_DELEGATED_SIGN_IN_APP_ACTION_CHANGES=true`, which applies to all of them. An Allow given through an API key, a Slack button or a delegated sign-in that isn't turned on never confirms: approvals now record who decided (`decision_by_kind`) and whether they were signed in to the app (`decision_by_signed_in_person`), both fixed with the decision. Calls that create, show, copy or rotate keys, passwords, tokens or other secrets, or carry one in their body, are left to the app unless the session has full access. Contract schemas mark secret fields with the new `secretValue(...)` helper (registry `secretValues`); the catalog generator records them, plus secret-sounding field names, as `secretFields` on catalog entries. Codex and SuperGrok device sign-in starts now have a response schema (`SubscriptionDeviceSignInStart`). A session with full access behaves as before. Scheduled, goal and other unattended turns, API-key and service turns, child sessions and service sessions gain nothing.

This is on by default for every organization, including existing ones: migration `0971` adds `chatActionsAllowed` (default true) to the agent admin-access setting. Owners and admins turn it off under Security & data > Agents, or with `PATCH /v1/organizations/:organizationId/agent-admin-access` (which now accepts either field). Embedding hosts that draw approvals with `ApprovalSurface` get the same card, with `approvalSurface.allow` and `approvalSurface.cancel` messages to translate; a host can leave the route off by not selecting these tools. The app-action catalog moved to `@opengeni/contracts/app-actions`. `SESSION_ADMIN_ACCESS_TOOL_NAMES` and `SESSION_ADMIN_ACCESS_TOOL_NAME_SET` are deprecated aliases of `SESSION_APP_ACTION_TOOL_NAMES` and `SESSION_APP_ACTION_TOOL_NAME_SET`. `toolReviewFields` takes an optional tool name.
