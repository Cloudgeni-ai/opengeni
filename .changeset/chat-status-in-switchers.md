The command menu's chats and the Control+Tab switcher show each chat's status with the sidebar's marks: working, queued, waiting, needs you (and for how long), failed, unread, scheduled, and who started it. Statuses show only while they are known to be current; chats outside the sidebar on screen are read again when a list opens and every 20 seconds while it stays open.
