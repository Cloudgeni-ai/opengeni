The Microsoft organization approval link now asks only for the Microsoft services the workspace offers. It covers the requested service plus the other Microsoft connections that share the same app registration, are rolled out to the workspace, and are allowed by the organization's integration policy. An organization that offers only Outlook Mail and Calendar no longer asks its IT administrator to grant OneDrive, SharePoint or contacts access; one approval still covers every offered service.
