Chats and workspaces across the whole account, from the keyboard. ⌘K now finds chats in all of your workspaces (this workspace's first, others tagged with their workspace) and finds workspaces by name; "Switch workspace…" lists them to pick from. Control+Tab in the desktop app reaches recent chats in other workspaces too. ⌥⌘↑ and ⌥⌘↓ (Ctrl+Alt+↑ and Ctrl+Alt+↓ on Windows and Linux) go to the previous and next workspace, and the workspace picker lists every workspace, grouped by organization, in that order. ⌘K and the desktop Chat menu also offer "Fork chat", which forks from the latest message.
