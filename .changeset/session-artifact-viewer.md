Clicking an image or file in a session's Artifacts panel opens a full-window viewer that steps through the listed artifacts in their current order, respecting search and filters, with Previous and Next buttons, Left and Right arrow keys, swipe on touch, an "N of M" position and Escape to close. Images fit the window and toggle to actual size on click, neighbouring images are preloaded, and other artifacts show their existing preview inside the viewer.
