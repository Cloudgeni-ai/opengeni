Products that embed the chat can give people one Send and nothing to learn. With `busySend: "steer"` (web `composerOptions`, native `useOpenGeniNativeSession`), a message sent while the agent works is read as soon as it can, and the stock screens show no Queue choice beside Send and no Queue or Steer words: paused or lined-up messages show as waiting messages, without Steer actions. `offerQueue: true` brings the Queue choice back. `SingleSendWording` applies the same wording around your own composition of the parts.
