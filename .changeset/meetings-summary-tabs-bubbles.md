The meeting page reads like notes. `MeetingDetailPage` has Summary · Transcript · Notepad tabs under the title, opening on Summary when the meeting has written notes and on Transcript otherwise; while the meeting is recorded on this device it keeps the single page with the recording bar, notepad and live preview. An unsaved edit of the Summary survives switching tabs, and changing `recordingId` starts the page fresh for that meeting. Transcript turns show the speaker's name once in their colour above a soft bubble, and a pencil on hover or focus opens "Correct the words" directly beside the ⋯ menu. The Summary shows `[Name](speaker:<label>)` and `[Name](person:<id>)` mentions as chips with the speaker's or person's current name (unknown ones read as plain text), and Copy copies the Markdown with names in place of mentions (code and images stay as written); the format is documented under Notes in `docs/recordings.md`. The standalone `MeetingNotes` shows mentions as their plain link text. A new optional `onAsk(recordingId, question)` adds "Ask about this meeting" under Summary and Transcript; resolve false to keep the question when the host couldn't take it. New English keys: `meetings.ask.couldNotAsk`, `meetings.ask.placeholder`, `meetings.ask.send`, `meetings.detailPage.show`, `meetings.detailPage.summary`, `meetings.notes.copied`, `meetings.notes.copy`, `meetings.notes.couldNotCopy` and `meetings.transcript.correctTheWordsOfNameAt`. The `@opengeni/react/testing/meetings` ready meeting's notes now use mentions.
