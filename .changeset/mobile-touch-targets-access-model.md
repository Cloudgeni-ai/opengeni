On phones, the session access control in the title bar and the Model and effort pill in the composer are now full 44×44 px touch targets. The title bar's narrow-width icon layout no longer shrinks the access control to 32 px on touch screens. The model pill no longer shrinks to a 28 px icon; when its row has less than 44 px of room, it steps aside instead.
