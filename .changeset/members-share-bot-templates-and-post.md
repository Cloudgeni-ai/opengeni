Regular members can do more without an admin. Anyone who can start chats in a workspace can now edit or delete its shared bot templates (only the creator or an admin can make one Only me). Members can record social posts through a connection they can see, and posts through workspace-owned connections still need workspace settings access. Organization admins, not only the owner, can now share a workspace's Slack bot with other workspaces when they manage that workspace's integrations.
