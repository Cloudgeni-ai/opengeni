Chats show who they wait on (`Session.waitingOn`) and their Linked items with live status (`listSessionLinks`, `addSessionLink`, `removeSessionLink`, and the `session.waiting_on.changed` and `session.links.changed` events). The workbench Changes tab folds each file and accepts optional `pullRequest` and `onOpenPullRequest` props for View on GitHub and Open PR; without them it is unchanged.
