`@opengeni/desktop` is published to npm, so a product can build its own branded desktop app on the kit without copying it from this repository. The package ships compiled JavaScript and types for its entry points (`@opengeni/desktop`, `/contract`, `/mac-login-agent`, `/preload` and `/preload/welcome`), and the welcome pages sit next to the contract entry as before. It joins the other packages' shared version line.
