The **PR reviewer** template posts one scannable review: a one-line verdict and short summary, each Critical and Bug finding as an inline comment on its lines with a concrete fix (a GitHub suggestion when it is small), nits left out or folded into a collapsed block, re-reviews that resolve fixed threads instead of repeating findings, and answers to questions in their own thread. Agents already created from the template keep their instructions.
