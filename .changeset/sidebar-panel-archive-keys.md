⌘B (Ctrl+B) toggles the sidebar, ⌥⌘B (Ctrl+Alt+B) toggles a chat's workspace panel, and ⌃⌘A (Ctrl+Alt+A) archives or restores the current chat. The sidebar and panel buttons and the chat menu show their keys. ⌃⌘S (Ctrl+Shift+S) still toggles the sidebar, and the menu bar shows it because rich text keeps ⌘B for bold.
