Compute hosts keep archiving on a slow or overloaded object store, and a busy disk no longer ends running VMs. An archive's uploads take one of a host-wide number of slots that follows what the store manages (one more per window stored in time, half as many when uploads time out), an upload that timed out or lost its connection is sent again after a pause, and uploads get two minutes per request instead of the half minute every request had. Before, dozens of 16 MiB pack uploads sent together to a slow store all ran past the 30 s deadline at once, nothing was stored and the archive started over, so idle sandboxes never reached the store and hosts never got their disk back. The agent's log lines now go to the journal from a thread of their own and its lease renewer runs on a thread and runtime of its own: when the journal stalled behind a saturated disk, the agent's threads waited in their log writes and held up the renewals with them until the guard froze and ended the host's VMs.
