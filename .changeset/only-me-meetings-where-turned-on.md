A person's Only me meeting works where they turn it on: in the workspace that stores it and their Personal workspace, plus the other workspaces they pick (`reachWorkspaceIds`, or `null` for all of them). New meetings start in those two; meetings from before keep showing everywhere they did. The meeting page offers it as **Available in…** when the host passes `reachWorkspaces`.
