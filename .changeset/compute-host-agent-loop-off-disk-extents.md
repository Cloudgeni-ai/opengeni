Compute hosts no longer stall under memory and disk pressure. The host agent read every sandbox disk's extent map (FIEMAP) on its loop once a second; on XFS that read waits for the allocation group's lock behind saves and uploads, so a pressed host answered nothing for up to a minute, its lease watchdog restarted the agent, and wakes were refused as `host_full` meanwhile. Saved and archived disks are now measured on a thread of their own when their file changed or every 30 seconds; a reading stands only for the same file, in the same generation (any step of the sandbox ending, or a capture of its disk, starts a new one) and for at most a minute. A wake checks that reading, or reads the disk, off the loop, waiting 100 ms at most and 200 ms in all over a couple of seconds; any disk without a reading books its whole bound. Running disks are read off the loop too: a sample waits 200 ms at most, uses the disks read by then and books the others' whole bound. The agent warns when one turn of its loop takes two seconds or more, naming what it was doing. Compute control lets the host group grow past `--scale-max` by one place for each draining member, so a host left draining no longer keeps a full group from growing.
