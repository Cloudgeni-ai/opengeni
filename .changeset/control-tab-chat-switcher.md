Add a Control+Tab recent chat switcher for the desktop app. A quick Control+Tab opens the previously viewed chat and pressing it again comes back; holding Control shows recently viewed chats in the middle of the window, Tab and Shift+Tab move the highlight, and releasing Control opens it. Browsers keep Control+Tab for their tabs, so the switcher only runs inside the desktop app. The prebuilt `@opengeni/react/compiled.css` includes its styles.
