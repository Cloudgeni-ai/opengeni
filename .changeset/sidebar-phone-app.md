Sidebar links to archived apps are kept when the layout changes, so restoring an app brings them back; only deleted apps are dropped. A person's own link to an app that became someone else's Only me is dropped on their next change. `sidebarLayoutIsOlder` tells a client when an answer is older than the layout it shows, and the `my-` id prefix and placement rules are documented.
