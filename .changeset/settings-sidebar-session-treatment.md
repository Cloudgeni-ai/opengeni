The settings sidebar now looks and behaves like the session sidebar. In the macOS app it shows the same window glass instead of a flat opaque panel, with no line between it and the window bar; it is as wide as the session sidebar, including a width you dragged it to; and the empty space beside the Opengeni logo moves the window. Both sidebars are drawn with the new shared `rail-surface` pieces (`ShellRail`, `RailSurface`, `RailBrandRow`, `RailBrandMark`, `RAIL_BRAND_LINK_CLASS`).
