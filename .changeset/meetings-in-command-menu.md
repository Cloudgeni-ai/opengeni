Meetings are part of the ⌘K menu. "Meetings" opens the Meetings page, the newest meetings are listed before anything is typed, and typing finds meetings by title, by someone who spoke, by words said in them, or by when they happened ("yesterday", "friday", "30 oct", "last week", also in Norwegian). On a meeting's page, the menu offers "Ask about this meeting", which asks the typed question as the meeting's Ask field does, and "Copy meeting link".
