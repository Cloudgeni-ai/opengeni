Boxes kept warm only by open session pages are now also bounded after admission. A box a turn, process or readiness hold kept warm becomes viewer-only when that holder leaves, and a viewer-only hold had no end. The sandbox reaper now drains a warm box whose every holder is a viewer and whose sessions have no open work after 30 minutes without use (`OPENGENI_SANDBOX_VIEWER_IDLE_DRAIN_MS`, `0` disables): the server measures use from viewer attaches and turns, and the web client extends it by reporting on each attach and heartbeat how long ago the person last used the page (`lastInteractionAgeMs`), so an active person with an open tab keeps their box. Only a person's own signed-in session can report interaction; the API ignores it from API keys (including personal ones) and delegated or service callers, so their viewers are bounded by server-observed activity. After an idle drain, a re-attach without newer interaction or session activity is refused, so an unattended page cannot keep a box warm; the person's next interaction re-attaches and the box resumes from its saved workspace. The reaper also drains the least recently active viewer-only boxes over `OPENGENI_SANDBOX_MAX_VIEWER_ONLY_BOXES_PER_WORKSPACE` or the plan tier's `viewerOnlyBoxesPerOrganization`, taking only boxes nobody used for five minutes. Drains are logged with their reason and counted in `opengeni_sandbox_viewer_only_drains_total{reason}`.

Migration 1478 grants the runtime the organization-wide sandbox counts from 1475, so the per-organization, per-member and running-box plan limits now apply at admission. Organizations that are not self-serve (the organization or one of its workspaces has an external source: embedding hosts, SDK and federated organizations) resolve to a new `embedded` plan tier, unbounded by default and configurable in `OPENGENI_SANDBOX_PLAN_LIMITS_JSON`; they are also outside the per-workspace viewer-only limit and the reaper's drains. The `restricted` tier now applies only while promotion enforcement is on. A new `new` tier covers Free self-serve organizations in their first three days (`organizationsYoungerThanMs` in the tier; 1 viewer-only box per organization and per member, 3 running boxes), and tiers apply whatever credential the caller uses. Joining a box that a live process or Browser/ComputerSession keeps warm no longer counts as one more viewer-only box, and a 429 for a computer limit gets its own message in the web client instead of the viewer-limit one.
