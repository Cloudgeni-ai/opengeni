Picking the wrong account no longer forces a sign-in. In the phone app, picking a signed-out account opens its sign-in over the account in use, so Cancel goes back. The account menus mark signed-out accounts. When the account in use is signed out, the sign-in screen lists the other accounts, so one tap returns to a signed-in one. In the desktop app, Cancel on the sign-in page returns to the account you came from, not the first signed-in one.
