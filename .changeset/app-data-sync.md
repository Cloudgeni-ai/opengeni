Add App data sync: `listAppSyncs`, `createAppSync`, `updateAppSync`, `deleteAppSync`, `runAppSync`, `setAppSyncOptIn` and `getAppSyncStatus` on the client, `site.data.syncs()` in Apps, and the `app_sync` scheduled task action. A sync fills an App's data from a connected tool on a schedule or event without a model run.
