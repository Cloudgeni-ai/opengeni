Meeting and dictation processing is measured stage by stage: CPU, memory and time of the programs each stage runs, provider wait, audio, list-price spend, credits and stored bytes, labelled by provider, model and who pays, plus failure, re-run and diarization signals and live preview usage. A new **Opengeni · Meetings & Transcription** Grafana dashboard prices it per audio hour at configurable cloud rates. The ffmpeg transcription segmenter accepts a `runCommand` so a host can account for each run.
