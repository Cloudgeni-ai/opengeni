Only me schedules can run on their owner's own connected machine. Saving the schedule is the owner's consent; each run gets a grant for exactly that run's chat, and every attempt rechecks that the owner is still a member, the machine is still theirs and the grant is still active. Workspace schedules and other people's schedules are still refused, and the schedule form locks Who can see it to Only me when a personal machine is picked.
