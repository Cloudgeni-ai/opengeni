Live transcripts on a model the deployment pays for now cost credits while credit billing is active: the payer is admitted before the live secret is minted (402 without credits, 403 `policy_blocked` when credits are turned off or the payer can't be identified), and each save charges the track's audio from its charged mark (`recording_tracks.live_charged_milliseconds`, migration 1101) to its streamed total at the model's voice price. The charge's usage receipt is recorded and the mark moved in the save's own transaction, so overlapping saves and retries charge each range once and a failed receipt fails the save for the recorder to retry; the debit follows the commit, and a failed one is applied from its receipt by the next voice admission. The duration is the recorder's report, recorded with basis `client_reported_duration`; a track charged in credits gets 4 live sessions, one more per 5 minutes of reported audio (at most 40), and none while the time since its first session runs more than 10 minutes plus a quarter ahead of what it reported (409 `live_grant_limit` / `live_streamed_behind`). `live.costUsd` stays the list price of everything streamed, and usage allowances that price unbilled use count only audio not charged in credits (`live_charged_cost_usd_micros`, and a same-signature `count_unbilled_model_allowance_debit()`), so charged audio never counts twice, also after a switch to an uncharged model or a write from the previous release. Live-transcript refusals have their own wording. A live model with no credit price isn't served then (503 `model_unavailable`, `retryable: false`). The deployment's Azure Speech account without a voice input MAI price (OPENGENI_VOICE_INPUT_MAI_PRICING_JSON) doesn't transcribe meetings while credits are billed: the default and automatic choice move to a model that can run (a priced deployment model, a subscription or the workspace's key), `GET …/recording-models` lists it `available: false` with `unavailableReason: "unpriced"`, and a meeting that names it fails with `model_unavailable`, which the app explains and offers another model for. Credit admission's own policy refusal (credits off, unidentified payer) is now a 403 `policy_blocked` on meeting transcripts too, not a 500. The SDK's live transcriber treats 402, 429 and any `retryable: false` refusal as final instead of reconnecting all meeting, and retries its last save when recording stops. Legacy billing accounts (OPENGENI_BILLING_LEGACY_ACCOUNT_IDS, CloudGeni) keep today's behaviour: no admission, no charge, no price shown on live-only models, and unpriced models still served.
