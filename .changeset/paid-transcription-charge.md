Live transcripts on a model the deployment pays for now cost credits while credit billing is active: the payer is admitted before the live secret is minted (402 without credits, 403 `policy_blocked` when credits are turned off or the payer can't be identified), and each save charges the track's audio from its charged mark (`recording_tracks.live_charged_milliseconds`, migration 1103) to its streamed total at the model's voice price. The charge's usage receipt is recorded and the mark moved in the save's own transaction, so overlapping saves and retries charge each range once and a failed receipt fails the save for the recorder to retry; the debit follows the commit, and a failed one is applied from its receipt when the meeting is finalized or by the next voice admission. The duration is the recorder's report, recorded with basis `client_reported_duration`. The SDK reports a track's streamed total about every minute while streaming (silence too), on pause and resume, and before asking for another session; a track charged in credits gets no more live sessions once the time between those reports (counted only up to a report of more audio, at most that audio plus a minute and 3 minutes each, so pauses never count) runs more than 10 minutes plus a quarter ahead of what it reported, once 4 sessions went unreported, or after 20 sessions plus one per 5 minutes reported (at most 100), or once the person has 4 hours in the last 24 hours, in that workspace, of time no reported audio accounts for (pauses, unreported sessions), counted only within an hour of each session's start since a provider session runs at most an hour, and stored per person and hour for tracks charged in credits only (`recording_live_unbacked_hours`, migration 1103; every API key of a workspace shares one bound, as keys record no person, while personal keys and delegated sessions count as their person) (409 `live_grant_limit` / `live_streamed_behind`); an honest recorder isn't refused for silence, long pauses, or quick pause and resume cycles (only a day with over about 4 hours of pauses shorter than a session loses the live preview), the SDK asks for no new live session while paused, and the React Native recorder now reports its pauses, resumes and interruptions to the live transcript. `live.costUsd` stays the list price of everything streamed, and usage allowances that price unbilled use count only audio not charged in credits (`live_charged_cost_usd_micros`, and a same-signature `count_unbilled_model_allowance_debit()`), so charged audio never counts twice, also after a switch to an uncharged model (audio saved through a previous-release pod during the rollout is the exception, documented). Live-transcript refusals have their own wording. A live model with no credit price isn't served then (503 `model_unavailable`, `retryable: false`). The deployment's Azure Speech account without a voice input MAI price (OPENGENI_VOICE_INPUT_MAI_PRICING_JSON) doesn't transcribe meetings while credits are billed: the default and automatic choice move to a model that can run (a priced deployment model, a subscription or the workspace's key), `GET …/recording-models` lists it `available: false` with `unavailableReason: "unpriced"`, and a meeting that names it fails with `model_unavailable`, which the app explains and offers another model for. Credit admission's own policy refusal (credits off, unidentified payer) is now a 403 `policy_blocked` on meeting transcripts too, not a 500. The SDK's live transcriber treats 402, 429 and any `retryable: false` refusal as final instead of reconnecting all meeting, and retries its last save when recording stops. Legacy billing accounts (OPENGENI_BILLING_LEGACY_ACCOUNT_IDS, CloudGeni) keep today's behaviour: no admission, no charge, no price shown on live-only models, and unpriced models still served.
