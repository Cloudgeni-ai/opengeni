Live transcripts on a model the deployment pays for now cost credits while credit billing is active: the payer is admitted before the live secret is minted (402 without credits, 403 `policy_blocked` when credits are turned off or the payer can't be identified), and each save charges the track's audio from its charged mark (`recording_tracks.live_charged_milliseconds`, migration 1101) to its streamed total at the model's voice price. The mark moves only once the charge is durable, so a failed charge is charged again by the next save. The duration is the recorder's report, recorded with basis `client_reported_duration`. Charged audio stores no list-price `live.costUsd`, so usage allowances that price unbilled use don't count it twice. A live model with no credit price isn't served then (503, `retryable: false`). The deployment's Azure Speech account without a voice input MAI price (OPENGENI_VOICE_INPUT_MAI_PRICING_JSON) doesn't transcribe meetings while credits are billed: the default and automatic choice move to a model that can run (a priced deployment model, a subscription or the workspace's key), `GET …/recording-models` lists it `available: false` with `unavailableReason: "unpriced"`, and a meeting that names it fails saying it isn't available. Credit admission's own policy refusal (credits off, unidentified payer) is now a 403 `policy_blocked` on meeting transcripts too, not a 500. The SDK's live transcriber treats 402, 429 and any `retryable: false` refusal as final instead of reconnecting all meeting. Legacy billing accounts (OPENGENI_BILLING_LEGACY_ACCOUNT_IDS, CloudGeni) keep today's behaviour: no admission, no charge, no price shown on live-only models, and unpriced models still served.
