Live transcripts on a model the deployment pays for now cost credits while credit billing is active: the payer is admitted before the live secret is minted (402 without credits, 403 `policy_blocked` when credits are turned off or the payer can't be identified), and each save charges the track's audio from its charged mark (`recording_tracks.live_charged_milliseconds`, migration 1122) to its streamed total at the model's voice price. The charge's usage receipt is recorded and the mark moved in the save's own transaction, so overlapping saves and retries charge each range once and a failed receipt fails the save for the recorder to retry; the debit follows the commit, and a failed one is applied from its receipt when the meeting is finalized or by the next voice admission. The duration is the recorder's report, recorded with basis `client_reported_duration`. The SDK reports a track's streamed total about every minute while streaming (silence too), on pause and resume, and before asking for another session; a track charged in credits gets no more live sessions once the time between those reports (counted for the track only up to a report of more audio, at most that audio plus a minute and 3 minutes each, so pauses never count) runs more than 10 minutes plus a quarter ahead of what it reported, once 4 sessions went unreported, or after 20 sessions plus one per 5 minutes reported (at most 100), or when the new session's hour would take the person's time no reported audio accounts for (pauses, unreported sessions, and time beyond the audio reported, without the track's minute of slack, so a trickle of audio doesn't cover it) in the last 24 hours in that workspace past 4 hours plus six times the live audio charged in credits (`LIVE_UNBACKED_PER_CHARGED`, so meetings of about 9 minutes or longer never use the day up and free provider time stays at most 4 hours plus six times what was paid), counted only within an hour of each session's start since a provider session runs at most an hour, with each session's whole hour counted up front and swapped at each report for what it actually left unaccounted plus what it could still run (so an abandoned session costs its hour, and one the provider never started gives it back), and stored per person and hour for tracks charged in credits only (`recording_live_unbacked_hours`, migration 1122; every API key of a workspace shares one bound, as keys record no person, while personal keys and delegated sessions count as their person; session requests of one bound, and saves on a session a previous release started, are serialized by an advisory lock, so a burst can't run over it; a session's time stays in the hour it began in, and hour rows are locked oldest first so concurrent requests can't deadlock) (409 `live_grant_limit` / `live_streamed_behind`); an honest recorder isn't refused for silence, long pauses, or quick pause and resume cycles (only a day with more pauses shorter than a session than the bound allows, or many live meetings under about 9 minutes, loses the live preview), the SDK asks for no new live session while paused, and the React Native recorder now reports its pauses, resumes and interruptions to the live transcript. `live.costUsd` stays the list price of everything streamed, and usage allowances that price unbilled use count only audio not charged in credits (`live_charged_cost_usd_micros`, and a same-signature `count_unbilled_model_allowance_debit()`), so charged audio never counts twice, also after a switch to an uncharged model (audio saved through a previous-release pod during the rollout is the exception, documented). Live-transcript refusals have their own wording. A live model with no credit price isn't served then (503 `model_unavailable`, `retryable: false`). The deployment's Azure Speech account without a voice input MAI price (OPENGENI_VOICE_INPUT_MAI_PRICING_JSON) doesn't transcribe meetings while credits are billed: the default and automatic choice move to a model that can run (a priced deployment model, a subscription or the workspace's key), `GET …/recording-models` lists it `available: false` with `unavailableReason: "unpriced"`, and a meeting that names it fails with `model_unavailable`, which the app explains and offers another model for. Credit admission's own policy refusal (credits off, unidentified payer) is now a 403 `policy_blocked` on meeting transcripts too, not a 500. The SDK's live transcriber treats 402, 429 and any `retryable: false` refusal as final instead of reconnecting all meeting, and retries its last save when recording stops. Legacy billing accounts (OPENGENI_BILLING_LEGACY_ACCOUNT_IDS, CloudGeni) keep today's behaviour: no admission, no charge, no price shown on live-only models, and unpriced models still served.
