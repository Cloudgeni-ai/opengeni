Meetings no longer say "Uploading" while a meeting is recorded or after Stop. Audio uploads while it records, so a meeting's page opened from Take notes now shows the recording's clock with Pause and Stop, and after Stop it reads Transcribing on the page and in the list. A paused recording reads Paused in the list, and the recorder's own line after Stop reads "Saving the last few seconds of audio." on web and phones. A meeting still being recorded elsewhere reads In progress; only a lost connection or a refused upload is called out. Adds `meetingHereStatus` to `@opengeni/react/recordings`; the list label for a recording in progress uses the new `meetings.recordings.inProgress` key instead of `meetings.recordings.uploading`, the recorder's `meetings.recorderPanel.uploadingCountPiecesOfAudio` and `finishingTheUpload` keys become `meetings.recorderPanel.savingTheLastFewSeconds`, and the meeting page's unused `meetings.detailPage.uploading*` and `stillUploading` keys are removed. Hosts that translate the catalog should rename their `meetings.recordings.uploading` translation to `meetings.recordings.inProgress`, add `savingTheLastFewSeconds` and drop the removed keys.
