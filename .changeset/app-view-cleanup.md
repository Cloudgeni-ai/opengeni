The app view is quieter: its header is one slim row on the page with no frame around the app (in the macOS app its controls join the window bar), Copy link lives only in the Published menu, the "…" keeps Pin, Duplicate, Reload, Settings and Archive, and the chat panel moves sides or opens as a full chat in one click. Publishing settings are in the address, so Back returns to the app, and Settings opened from the app goes back to it. An app's Settings use one row style, with search engines, reports and takedown, duplicate, export, resetting allowed actions and archive under a collapsed More; everyone who can open an app sees its Settings.
