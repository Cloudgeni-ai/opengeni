The app view is quieter: its header is one slim row on the page with no frame around the app (in the macOS app its controls join the window bar), Copy link lives only in the Published menu, the "…" keeps full screen, Reload, Pin, Settings and Archive, and the chat panel moves sides, opens as a full chat or hides in one click. A hidden chat leaves no strip: the app takes the whole width and Show chat sits in its header. Whether the chat is open is remembered per person and app, opening by default only for apps you have chatted with or reach from a chat. Publishing settings are in the address, so Back returns to the app, and Settings opened from the app goes back to it. An app's Settings use one row style, with search engines and reports collapsed in the publish form and duplicate, export, resetting allowed actions and archive under a collapsed More; everyone who can open an app sees its Settings.
