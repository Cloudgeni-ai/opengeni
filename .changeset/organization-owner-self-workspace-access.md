Organization owners and admins can now remove their own access to a shared workspace from the organization settings, the same way they already grant or change it, including workspaces an organization API key created for an embedded tenant. They can also list, add, change, and remove members from any shared workspace's own Members page, whether or not they hold a role in that workspace. The organization Workspaces list and each person's Workspace access editor gain a name search for organizations with many shared workspaces.
