The in-chat connect card on mobile now offers your own login the way the web card does. When you already have exactly one login of your own for the service and it isn't set up in this workspace yet, the card's main action is "Use my <service> here". One tap turns the connector on for each person's own account, turns your login on here if it was off, and adds it to the conversation, with no new sign-in and no "Who can use this connection?" question. "Connect an account for everyone instead" and "Connect a different account" stay as secondary choices.
