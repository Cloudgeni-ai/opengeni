Auto-connect (MCP Enterprise-Managed Authorization) in both directions. Organization owners and admins can trust Claude's per-organization issuer (Claude Team and Enterprise) or their identity provider under Organization settings → Connected agents → Auto-connect, and members' agents are then connected to the organization MCP server the first time they use it, with no sign-in or consent screen: `/oauth/token` redeems ID-JAGs with the JWT bearer grant, members are matched by verified email to active members, the issuer's access setting applies to everyone it connects, and Recent attempts say why one was refused. The authorization server metadata now advertises the grant, the ID-JAG profile and Client ID Metadata Document support, and clients may be named by their metadata document URL. In the other direction, a new organization connector sign-in, "Connect everyone automatically" (`login: "auto_connect"`), gives each member their own account in an outside MCP app that trusts the organization's Opengeni issuer (`/v1/organizations/:id/identity`), signed and redeemed before their turn or when they choose Connect, and renewed without refresh tokens; admins check support before adding and Test as themselves. Opengeni's client metadata document lists the JWT bearer grant. New SDK methods: `listMcpTrustedIssuers`, `checkMcpTrustedIssuer`, `createMcpTrustedIssuer`, `updateMcpTrustedIssuer`, `deleteMcpTrustedIssuer`, `listMcpTrustedIssuerEvents`, `checkOrganizationConnectorAutoConnect`, `testOrganizationConnectorAutoConnect` and `connectWorkspaceOrganizationConnector`.
