Work-log steps whose text is cut off now open to show it in full, selecting their text no longer toggles them, and an open step has a Copy button. A chat's or Site's title can be copied by right-clicking it in the top bar (or the macOS window bar), and from the chat's "…" menu. Site rows and the Site page now offer what fits from a chat's actions plus the Site's own: Copy link, Copy title, Pin, Duplicate, Site details, Open published Site, Archive and Move to project on the row, and Copy title, Pin, Duplicate, Site details and Archive in a new "…" on the Site page.
