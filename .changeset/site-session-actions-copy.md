Work-log steps whose text is cut off now open to show it in full, selecting their text no longer toggles them, and an open step has a Copy button. A chat's or Site's title can be copied by right-clicking it in the top bar (or a Site's title in the macOS window bar), and from the chat's "…" menu. Site rows and the Site page now offer what fits from a chat's actions plus the Site's own: Copy link, Copy title, Pin, Duplicate, Site details, Archive, Open published Site (once it is on the web) and Move to project on the row, and Copy title, Duplicate and Site details in a new "…" on the Site page, with Pin and Archive there too while the sidebar is open.
