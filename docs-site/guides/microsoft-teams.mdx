The Opengeni app for Microsoft Teams lets you give tasks to your Opengeni
agents from Teams. Send Opengeni a message in a 1:1 chat and it works on the
task with your Opengeni access, then replies in the same chat. Where your
organization has turned them on, you can also mention **@Opengeni** in a group
chat or a channel.

Opengeni is an AI agent. Its replies are generated by AI and can be wrong, so
check them before you rely on them.

## Requirements

- **An Opengeni account** in the Opengeni organization your company uses. You
  can create one at [app.opengeni.ai](https://app.opengeni.ai). Installing the
  Teams app does not create an account.
- **Your organization connected once.** An administrator connects your
  Microsoft 365 organization to a shared Opengeni workspace. See
  [Connect your organization](#connect-your-organization).
- **Your own Teams account linked** to your Opengeni account. See
  [Link your account](#link-your-account).
- **Where it works.** 1:1 chats work as soon as your organization is connected
  and your account is linked. Group chats and channels work only where they
  have been turned on for your organization; email
  [support@opengeni.ai](mailto:support@opengeni.ai) to ask about them.

## Connect your organization

Each Microsoft 365 organization is connected once, to one shared Opengeni
workspace. The person who connects it needs:

- the **Global Administrator** or **Teams Administrator** role in Microsoft 365,
  and
- an Opengeni account that is an administrator of the shared workspace that
  Teams should use.

To connect:

1. In Teams, choose **Apps**, search for **Opengeni** and choose **Add**. If
   your organization restricts apps, a Teams administrator first allows
   Opengeni in the Teams admin center (**Teams apps** > **Manage apps**).
2. Send Opengeni any message in a 1:1 chat. It replies with a button to
   connect your organization.
3. Choose the button and sign in to Opengeni.
4. Sign in with your Microsoft work account when asked. Opengeni checks that it
   is the same account you use in Teams and that it has one of the
   administrator roles above.
5. Choose the shared workspace Teams should use, and confirm.

Your organization is now connected and your own Teams account is linked to
your Opengeni account. Everyone else links their own account the first time
they message Opengeni.

People who message Opengeni before their organization is connected get a short
reply asking them to contact their Microsoft 365 or Teams administrator.

## Link your account

1. In Teams, open a 1:1 chat with Opengeni and send a message.
2. Choose **Link account**. Opengeni opens in your browser.
3. Sign in to Opengeni if asked, check the workspace, and choose
   **Link my account**.
4. Go back to Teams and send your request again.

The link connects exactly the Teams account that opened it to the Opengeni
account you are signed in with. If the link has expired or was already used,
send Opengeni a new message to get a new one. To link from a group chat or
channel, message Opengeni directly first; it never posts account links in shared
conversations.

## Use Opengeni in Teams

- **1:1 chats.** Send a message describing what you need. When a new task
  starts, Opengeni replies with a link to follow it in Opengeni, then sends its
  answer in Teams when it finishes. Your 1:1 chats are private to you. They are
  saved in your Personal workspace in Opengeni when your organization uses
  Personal workspaces, and otherwise in the connected workspace, visible only
  to you.
- **Group chats and channels.** Mention **@Opengeni**. Only messages that
  mention it start work. In a channel, Opengeni replies in the thread, and a
  later mention in the same thread continues the same task. Everyone in a group
  chat must have linked their account and have access to the connected
  workspace, or Opengeni does not start work there. These chats are saved in
  the connected workspace and are visible to its members.
- **Approvals and questions.** When a task needs your approval or an answer,
  Opengeni sends a link to the task in Opengeni. Approve or answer there, signed
  in to your Opengeni account; this is never done in Teams.

Opengeni uses the same tools, connections and Knowledge in Teams as in the
Opengeni app, within your own access.

## Commands

Type a command in a 1:1 chat with Opengeni, or pick it from the command menu
above the message box.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `help` | Shows what Opengeni can do in Teams and how to get started, without starting a task. |
| `disconnect` | Unlinks your Teams account from your Opengeni account, after you confirm. |

## AI-generated replies and feedback

Opengeni's replies in Teams are written by AI and are labelled as AI-generated.
Where Teams shows feedback buttons on a reply, use them to tell us whether the
reply helped. You can also email [support@opengeni.ai](mailto:support@opengeni.ai)
to report a wrong, harmful or unexpected reply; include the time and the chat
it was in.

## Your data

What Opengeni keeps from Teams:

- **Your account link.** Your Microsoft 365 organization (tenant) ID and your
  Microsoft Entra object ID, linked to your Opengeni account.
- **Messages to Opengeni and its replies.** A message you send to Opengeni, or a
  mention of it, and Opengeni's replies become part of an Opengeni chat and are
  kept like any other Opengeni chat. Your message is sent to the AI model that
  runs the task, as described in the [privacy policy](https://opengeni.ai/privacy).
- **Delivery records** of each Teams message Opengeni received and each reply it
  sent, so that it never starts a task twice or sends a reply twice, and so a
  missing reply can be investigated.
- **Conversation context, in group chats and channels where it is turned on.**
  So that a mention can be answered with what was said before it, Opengeni
  keeps the recent messages people post there: the message text, the sender's
  display name and Microsoft Entra object ID, and the time. It keeps at most
  the newest 100 messages per channel thread or group chat and never uses
  messages older than seven days; older ones are deleted. When a message is
  edited in Teams, Opengeni keeps the new text, and when it is deleted,
  Opengeni deletes it too. When someone joins a group chat, the messages kept
  for that chat are deleted. Files and images are never downloaded, and
  messages from 1:1 chats, from bots, and from chats where Opengeni is not
  turned on are never kept this way. Messages that were used to answer a
  mention become part of that Opengeni chat.

The Teams app does not read your other Teams messages, your files or your
Microsoft 365 data. A separate **Microsoft Teams** integration, which each
person can connect in Opengeni, lets agents read and send Teams messages with
that person's Microsoft permissions; it is set up and removed independently of
the Teams app.

Opengeni does not use your Teams data for advertising and does not train AI
models on it. See the [privacy policy](https://opengeni.ai/privacy) for how
Opengeni handles personal data, where it is stored, and the providers that
process it.

## Unlink, disconnect or remove the app

- **Unlink your account.** Send `disconnect` in your 1:1 chat with Opengeni, or
  in Opengeni open **Capabilities** in the connected workspace, find
  **Opengeni for Teams** and choose **Unlink account**. Opengeni stops accepting
  work from your Teams account until you link it again, and replies that were
  not yet sent are dropped. Your Opengeni chats stay in Opengeni.
- **Disconnect your organization.** A workspace administrator opens
  **Capabilities** > **Opengeni for Teams** and chooses **Disconnect bot**.
  Opengeni stops accepting work from your organization's Teams, cancels pending
  work, and deletes the conversation context it kept. Existing Opengeni chats
  stay. Reconnecting uses the same workspace.
- **Remove the app from Teams.** In Teams, choose **Apps** > **Manage your
  apps**, find Opengeni and choose **Uninstall**, or remove it from a group
  chat or team in that conversation's apps settings. A Teams administrator can
  remove or block it for the whole organization in the Teams admin center.
  Removing the app does not delete your data in Opengeni: unlink or disconnect
  first, and delete chats in Opengeni or ask
  [support@opengeni.ai](mailto:support@opengeni.ai) to delete your data.

## Limitations

- Opengeni reads only the text of Teams messages. Files, images and other
  attachments sent in Teams are not read; attach files in the Opengeni app
  instead.
- Approvals and private questions are answered in Opengeni, not in Teams.
- Group chats and channels work only where they have been turned on for your
  organization, and only for messages that mention Opengeni. Group chats can
  have at most 250 people, all with linked accounts.
- Conversation context covers standard channels and group chats only, not
  private or shared channels, and never messages posted before it was turned
  on.
- One Microsoft 365 organization connects to one Opengeni workspace. Moving it
  to another workspace is not supported.
- Long replies may arrive as several Teams messages.
- If Teams did not confirm that a reply arrived, Opengeni does not send it
  again, so it cannot appear twice. Open the task link to see the full answer in
  Opengeni.

## Troubleshooting

- **Opengeni doesn't reply at all.** Your organization may not be connected yet,
  or Opengeni may not be turned on for that group chat or channel. Ask your
  Teams administrator, or email support.
- **Opengeni keeps asking you to link your account.** Open the link from your
  latest 1:1 message with Opengeni and finish linking, signed in to the
  Opengeni account that has access to the connected workspace.
- **"I couldn't verify this chat's access."** Everyone in the group chat needs a
  linked Opengeni account and access to the connected workspace.
- **"Opengeni could not start this task."** Check that you still have access to
  the connected workspace and can start chats there.
