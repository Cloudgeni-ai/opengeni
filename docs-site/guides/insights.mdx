description: "See what your agents cost and why: spend, model calls, and tokens for a workspace or the whole organization."
---

Insights shows where usage goes. Open it from **Workspace settings → Insights** (workspace admins) or **Organization settings → Insights** (anyone who can read billing). The organization view adds a workspace filter and breakdown.

## What you see

- **Spend.** Credits charged, plus an estimate (marked `~`) for calls paid by a connected plan such as ChatGPT or Claude, or by your own API key.
- **Model calls, tokens, and cache hit rate.**
- **Tokens and cost by type.** New input, cache reads, cache writes, and output, each with its share of cost.
- **Over time.** Hourly for today, daily otherwise, optionally stacked by model.
- **Breakdown.** Group by model, provider, payment source, workspace, project, session, person, schedule, or source. Select a row to drill in. **Recent calls** lists individual calls.

Pick a period and add filters; the whole view is in the URL, so you can share it.

## Privacy

Totals include everyone's usage. Other people's **Only me** chats and Personal workspaces show as one amount per person, with no title or link.
