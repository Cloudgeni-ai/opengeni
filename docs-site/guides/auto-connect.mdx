---
title: "Auto-connect"
description: "Connect everyone's AI apps to Opengeni, and Opengeni to your organization's MCP apps, with no sign-in screens."
icon: "plug"
---

Auto-connect skips the sign-in and consent screen when someone connects an AI
app. An identity issuer your organization already trusts vouches for the
person with a short-lived signed assertion (an ID-JAG), and the app on the
other side trusts that issuer. It follows the MCP
[Enterprise-Managed Authorization](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/extensions/auth/enterprise-managed-authorization)
extension and works in both directions:

- **Into Opengeni:** people in your Claude Team or Enterprise organization, or
  people who sign in through your identity provider, have their agent
  connected to Opengeni the first time they use it.
- **Out of Opengeni:** an organization connector set to connect everyone
  automatically gives each member their own account in an outside MCP app,
  with no sign-in, the first time they use it.

Owners and admins set it up. Members don't need to do anything.

## Connect Claude to Opengeni

You need to be an owner or admin in Opengeni and in Claude.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start in Opengeni">
    Open **Organization settings → Connected agents**, choose **Set up auto-connect**, then
    **Claude**. Keep this page open: it shows the **Server URL** and **Tenant ID** to copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Opengeni to Claude">
    In Claude's organization settings, add a custom connector with the **Server URL**. Open the
    connector and set up **Managed authorization**, choose **Claude-issued**, and enter the **Tenant
    ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trust Claude's issuer">
    Claude then shows an **Issuer URL**, unique to your Claude organization. Paste it into **Issuer
    URL** in Opengeni. Opengeni checks it right away and shows how many signing keys it found.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose access and turn it on">
    Choose what each person's agent can do and in which workspaces, then **Turn on auto-connect**.
    Back in Claude, run the connector's test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From then on, the first time someone in your Claude organization uses
Opengeni there, Claude connects them as themselves. Opengeni matches them by
their verified email to an active member of your organization, so they need
an Opengeni account in it first; auto-connect never creates accounts or adds
members. Each connection appears in **Connected agents** as
**Auto-connected**.

<Note>
  Claude-issued auto-connect is in beta at Anthropic, on Claude Team and Enterprise plans.
</Note>

## Connect through your identity provider

Choose **Okta** or **Other** instead of Claude. Copy the **Server URL**,
**Tenant ID** and **Audience** into your identity provider's setup for
Opengeni (in Okta, Cross App Access with the Server URL as the resource), and
paste the provider's issuer URL into Opengeni exactly as it publishes it.

Under **Advanced** you can:

- allow only some apps, by their client IDs, one per line;
- give a key set URL, when the issuer's keys can't be found from its issuer
  URL.

## Manage an issuer

Open an issuer from **Connected agents** to:

- change what agents connected through it can do, and where. The change
  applies to everyone it already connected;
- see **Recent attempts**: each app and person that connected, or the reason
  one was refused, such as an email with no matching member;
- turn it off or remove it, which disconnects everyone it connected.

Disconnecting one auto-connected agent works like any other, but the app
connects it again the next time the person uses it. To stop that, turn off
the issuer or remove the person from the organization.

## Connect everyone to an outside app

Some MCP apps accept assertions from issuers they trust. For those, an
organization connector can connect every member automatically:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the connector">
    Open **Organization settings → Integrations**, add a connector and choose **Connect everyone
    automatically**. Opengeni checks that the app supports it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trust Opengeni in the app">
    The connector's page shows your organization's **Issuer URL** and Opengeni's **Client ID**.
    Enter them in the app's settings for trusted (ID-JAG) issuers. If the app gave you its own
    client ID for Opengeni, enter that in Opengeni instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Choose **Test as you**. Opengeni connects you without saving anything and shows the app's answer
    when it refuses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each member then gets their own account the first time an agent uses the app,
or when they choose **Connect**. If the app refuses someone, they see the
usual sign-in instead.

## How it stays safe

- An assertion is accepted only when it is signed by an issuer your
  organization turned on, names Opengeni as its audience and your
  organization as its tenant, and matches the app that sends it. Each
  assertion is short-lived and works once.
- People are matched by verified email to existing members only.
- There are no refresh tokens: access ends when the issuer stops vouching,
  the issuer is turned off, or the person leaves the organization.
- Opengeni's own issuer has a separate signing key per organization, kept
  encrypted, and signs only for members with a verified email.
