The SuperOffice CRM connection lets agents read and update companies,
contacts, sales, projects, appointments and tickets in SuperOffice CRM Online.
It uses SuperOffice's own MCP server, `https://mcp.superoffice.com/mcp`, and
each person signs in with their own SuperOffice user, so agents see only what
that user can see.

## Connect

1. Open **Capabilities**, find **SuperOffice CRM** and press **Connect**, or
   ask an agent in chat to connect SuperOffice.
2. Sign in to SuperOffice and approve access.

Reading needs a CRM Suite Plus license; updating needs CRM Suite Super.

## Approvals

Every read runs without asking. The six update tools (`update_company`,
`update_person`, `update_sale`, `update_project`, `update_appointment` and
`update_ticket`) ask first. Change this under the connection's **Tool
permissions**.

## If Connect fails

The connect dialog shows the reason SuperOffice gave. "This service only
accepts sign-ins from apps it has approved" means SuperOffice has not allowed
Opengeni's sign-in address for your environment; ask SuperOffice support to
allow Opengeni.

See SuperOffice's [MCP documentation](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/ai/learn/mcp).
